Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find my purchased Office software?

Sign in to the Microsoft account associated with your purchase and check the subscriptions or products section.

Can I install Microsoft 365 on a new computer?

Your ability to install Office depends on your subscription and its device limits. Check your Microsoft account for available installation options.
